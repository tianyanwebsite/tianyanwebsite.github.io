Pro-angiogenesis

ANGEOO Therapeutics is developing next-generation pro-angiogenic and anti-angiogenic therapies for diseases driven by abnormal blood vessel formation and function.

By either promoting or inhibiting the formation of new blood vessels, we aim to restore vascular health and enable new treatment options for conditions ranging from ischemic and degenerative diseases to cancer, where abnormal angiogenesis fuels tumor growth and metastasis.

Despite decades of progress in angiogenesis research, current therapies remain limited by efficacy and safety challenges. ANGEOO is developing innovative pro-angiogenic and anti-angiogenic strategies designed to deliver more effective, precise, and safer treatments for patients.
